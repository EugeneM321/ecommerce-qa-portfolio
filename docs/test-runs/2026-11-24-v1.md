## Release decision rationale

- Cart, checkout, and the paid order agreed on $29 for two items, including $5 shipping once.
- Decline and cancellation created no paid order and left both the cart and stock intact.
- Interrupted checkout and reload recovery returned one paid order for the original attempt. The observed stock sequence was 5 before acceptance, 3 after acceptance, and 3 after recovery.
- The shopper saw an unconfirmed state with a clear retry action, followed by an explicit paid confirmation for the same attempt.

Smoke, release-critical, and broader regression cases passed. The browser and persisted state agreed on the [release gate](../risk-and-release-gate.md). No reproducible product defects or unresolved findings were observed. This decision applies to the synthetic Version 1 build and the manual scope.

## Change-impact review

The [asynchronous confirmation note](../change-impact.md) still applies. The baseline records payment and the stock deduction at acceptance. Interrupted confirmation withholds the shopper-facing result, which retry recovers for the same attempt. A future asynchronous design would still need distinct pending and final-state checks, coverage for delayed and repeated delivery, and checks that refresh or retry preserves the attempt and prevents duplicate effects. The note was reviewed without changes.
